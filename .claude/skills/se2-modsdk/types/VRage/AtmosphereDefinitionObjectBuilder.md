# AtmosphereDefinitionObjectBuilder

`VRage:Keen.VRage.Render.Data.AtmosphereDefinitionObjectBuilder`

- bundle: **VRage**
- namespace: `Keen.VRage.Render.Data`
- definitions using it: **8**

## Fields

Straight from the SDK's own XML documentation — this is the only authoritative description of what each field means.

| field | meaning |
|---|---|
| `AmbientDiffuseFactor` | Adds depth to the ambient light. Too low values make shadowed areas look overly flat whereas too high values will lead to discoloration. May not be visible if raytracing or environment probes are simulating this. |
| `AmbientIntensity` | Controls the intensity of approximated ambient lighting in areas in the shade, but receiving light from the sky. May not be visible if raytracing or environment probes are simulating this. |
| `AmbientTintFactor` | Tint the approximated ambient lighting in areas in the shade, but receiving light from the sky. May not be visible if raytracing or environment probes are simulating this. |
| `AtmosphereBoundingHeight` | The bounding height of the atmosphere. Determines the cutoff point for atmosphere rendering. Proportionate to planet radius. If this value is too small the atmosphere will get cut off, if it is too high it will lower the quality of the atmosphere simulation. Set this value to the lowest value that does not cut off the Rayleigh, Mie and Night Fog layers when viewed from space. |
| `BetaMieScatteringColor` | Color of the Mie scattering. Mie scattering is seen mostly around the sun as a halo effect, and is brighter at sunset. |
| `BetaMieScatteringIntensity` | Intensity of the Mie scattering. Controls the effective "density" of the Mie atmosphere and makes the glow around the sun brighter. |
| `BetaRayleighScatteringColor` | The color of the Rayleigh scattering. The R, G and B components of this correspond to the amount each component is scattered. The sky will tend towards higher components while sunsets will tend towards lower components. The sunset gradient tends to go from: highest component (sky) middle component (between sky and sunset) lowest component (deep sunset). |
| `BetaRayleighScatteringIntensity` | The intensity of the Rayleigh scattering. Controls the effective "density" of the Rayleigh atmosphere. Affects the intensity and color of sunsets, and the intensity and color of the sky. |
| `CameraMovementEffect` | Effect to play when the player is moving above certain speed to give a sense of motion. See CameraMovementEffectsComponent |
| `DesaturationFactor` | Reduce the saturation of the atmosphere, causing it to be grayer, for artistic effect. |
| `DirectLightScatterMult` | Controls the intensity of sunsets as they affect the color of direct sunlight hitting the ground. |
| `FogIntensitySpace` | Controls the amount of atmospheric rayleigh "fog" on the terrain, viewed from space. Increase to make the atmosphere color over the planet appear thicker. |
| `FogIntensitySurface` | Controls the amount of atmospheric "fog" on the terrain, viewed from the surface. Increase to make the atmospheric perspective (mountains appearing sky colored in the distance) appear thicker when on the ground. |
| `ForceIgnoringAtmosphereInVolumetricRendering` | Hotfix for correctly handling volumetric rendering and atmosphere rendering order. Do not touch unless you know what you are doing. TODO SE2-30463 - MBOIT will solve the issue, remove it then. |
| `HeightScaleMie` | Scale the height of the Mie atmosphere. Will make the glow brighter as well as making it happen at higher elevations. A lower height has the scattering during sunsets more intense than the scattering during midday. Proportionate to planet radius. Usually much shorter than HeightScaleRayleigh. You may need to adjust AtmosphereBoundingHeight to avoid cutoff when viewed from space. |
| `HeightScaleNightFog` | Height of the glowing night fog atmosphere layer. Proportionate to planet radius. You may need to adjust AtmosphereBoundingHeight to avoid cutoff when viewed from space. |
| `HeightScaleRayleigh` | Scale the height of the Rayleigh atmosphere. Proportionate to planet radius. If this is too short, the sky might be too thin looking straight up but too scattered looking to the horizon. If this is too tall, sunsets might be overly weak, but this can be mitigated with SunsetMultiplier. You may need to adjust AtmosphereBoundingHeight to avoid cutoff when viewed from space. |
| `Intensity` | Artificially increase the amount of light generated by the scattering simulation. Useful if stars are too visible during the day because the sky is not bright enough. |
| `MieG` | Width of the angle of Mie scattering. Controls how tight the mie glow fits around the sun. Higher values are tighter. Most particles in the real universe have a value very close to 1. |
| `NightFogColor` | Color of glowing night fog, seen at night. Used to increase visibility of terrain. |
| `NightFogIntensity` | Intensity of glowing night fog. |
| `RayleighTint` | Tint the entire rayleigh layer. This will tint the color of both the sky, and sunsets, but not affect the scattering simulation outside of that. |
| `SunDiscScatterMult` | Controls the amount a sunset affects the color/brightness of the visible sun. |
| `SunlightPlanetScale` | Allows a planet to act like a larger planet for the purpose of sunsets. Used to make the planet atmosphere simulate a real one, with a much larger atmosphere, more realistically. Also affects the range that sunsets occur in. |
| `SunsetMultiplier` | Intensifies scattering of sunsets. Use this if sunsets are not vibrant enough, but you don't want to change the appearance of the sky when a sunset is not occuring. |
| `UndergroundFalloffRadius` | Radius underground, over which the rayleigh additive light fades out. Makes sure that large underground caverns don't have atmospheric fog. |
